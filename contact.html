Frequently Asked Questions

How long does editing typically take?

Turnaround time depends on project complexity. Starter packages: 3-5 days. Professional: 5-7 days. Premium: 7-10 days. Rush options available.

Do you offer revisions?

Yes! Each package includes revision rounds. Starter: 1 round. Professional: 2 rounds. Premium: 3 rounds. Additional revisions available for a fee.

What file formats do you deliver?

We deliver in multiple formats: MP4, MOV, ProRes, DCP, and more. We optimize for your specific needs and platform requirements.

Can you work with my raw footage?

Absolutely! We work with all formats and codecs. Share your footage via cloud storage (Google Drive, Dropbox, WeTransfer), or we can provide FTP access.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. We typically request 50% upfront to start the project and 50% upon completion. Custom payment terms available for larger projects.

How do we communicate during the project?

We maintain regular communication via email, WhatsApp, or your preferred method. Progress updates and draft reviews are provided throughout.
